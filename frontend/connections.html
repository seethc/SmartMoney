This setup uses production account data, never sandbox balances. Enable Banking handles the bank connection; SmartMoney stores a local copy. No payment operations are available.

2. Register with Enable Banking

Not configured

Open the Enable Banking control panel ↗, sign up, and register an application. Select PRODUCTION and Account Information (AIS). Upload the public certificate from step 1, then enter this exact redirect URL:

Activate restricted production access by linking your own bank account to the application in the provider control panel. Complete its terms and bank consent yourself. Personal use is subject to the provider’s terms and linked-account restrictions. If registration asks for details unavailable to a personal project, stop and bring that screen back here.

Never enter your bank password, PIN, recovery codes, or provider private key in this form. If the provider declines the configured redirect, bring the message back here; do not expose this server publicly.

3. Choose a bank and approve access

The list comes from the provider’s current UK personal-account API. Availability of Barclays, Santander, Revolut, Chase and your particular Ulster savings product must be checked here and in the bank consent screen.

Continue to Enable Banking, then approve access in your bank’s own website or app. The consent requests balances and transactions for up to 90 days. Use the same browser for the complete consent flow and start at the configured app address so the return URL and browser cookie match.
