Privacy information

Personal installation · 23 September 2026

Who uses this installation

SmartMoney is a self-hosted personal finance application operated by the owner of this installation for their own accounts. It is not a public service for other people's financial data. This notice describes the personal-use configuration; review it before changing how the application is operated or giving other people access.

The operator and data protection contact is the account owner identified by the contact email supplied in this installation's Enable Banking application registration. Use that contact for questions about the local records. This application is separate from Enable Banking, Tailscale and your bank.

Information used and why

With your bank consent, SmartMoney retrieves supported account identifiers and labels, balances, booked transaction dates, descriptions, amounts and counterparties through Enable Banking. It also stores the account preferences, categories, planned payments and CSV records that you enter. It uses these records to show spending, income, forecasts and suggested transfers. Suggestions never execute financial transactions.

Where information goes

The connection is bank → Enable Banking → your Python backend on the Raspberry Pi → your authorised browser or installed web app. CSV uploads go to that backend. Private remote access uses Tailscale. Bank authentication takes place through the bank/provider flow; SmartMoney does not ask for or store your bank password or PIN.

Enable Banking, your bank and Tailscale process information involved in their services under their own notices. Read the notices shown during their registration and consent processes. SmartMoney itself does not include advertising, analytics trackers, or calls that send your financial records to a cloud AI service.

Local storage and access

Financial records are stored in SQLite on the Pi. Those databases are not encrypted by SmartMoney. Bank-connection private keys and consent sessions are kept in an authenticated encrypted vault, with its encryption key held separately. The configured private HTTPS deployment also uses an app password. Authorised devices and anyone with sufficient access to the Pi or its backups may be able to read your records; protect those devices and backups.

The browser remembers your selected workspace in local storage. A short-lived cookie binds bank consent to the browser that started it. The browser may retain app-login credentials according to its own settings. The app does not deliberately cache financial API data for offline viewing.

Retention, corrections and disconnection

Records remain until the owner removes them from the installation and any backups; there is no automatic retention period. Categories and planning preferences can be corrected in the app. Some changes require administration of the local database.

Disconnecting a bank session revokes that provider session but retains previously imported local history. You can also revoke access with your bank or Enable Banking. Stopping or uninstalling the service alone does not erase database files or backups. For complete deletion, the owner must revoke active consents and deliberately remove the relevant server records and backups.

Changes to this notice

Review this notice whenever data sources, hosting, access or app features change. It describes this personal prototype and does not claim that it is suitable for operating a public financial service.
