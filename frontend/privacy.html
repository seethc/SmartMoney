Personal installation

The owner operates this private dashboard for their own financial records. SmartMoney is independent of Snoop and your banks.

Screenshot processing

Images you select are sent only to this SmartMoney server. Local Tesseract OCR extracts draft text, balances and spending categories without cloud services or LLMs. Images and OCR text are held in memory while processing and reviewing, and are not intentionally written to disk. Clear the draft or leave the page to discard the browser preview. Operating system swap or diagnostic dumps are outside the app's control.

Saved records

Confirmed balances are saved with an audit of prior values and dates. Reviewed spending summaries are stored separately from transaction history. The app also stores account preferences, CSV transaction history, classifications and planned payments in SQLite on the server. These databases are not encrypted by the app. Protect your devices and use encrypted backups.

Access and retention

Private remote access uses your configured HTTPS service and app password. Your browser remembers the selected workspace and may remember login credentials. Financial API data is not cached for offline use. Records remain until the owner removes them and their backups; stopping the service does not erase data.

External services

Snoop connects to your banks separately under its own terms. SmartMoney does not connect to Snoop or your bank, and has no bank credentials or payment access. Tailscale may provide your network access. Old credentials left by a previous installation are not loaded by this version; revoke any old bank consents separately.
