Open Snoop’s account overview and check its last refresh time. Capture the account names and GBP balances. Scroll and take more screenshots if needed. Avoid spending charts, totals and transaction lists.

Reading happens on your SmartMoney server, which discards images and text after processing. The review draft stays in this browser until you save or discard it. Only confirmed balances are saved. This does not connect to Snoop or move money.

Checking local screenshot reader…

PNG, JPEG or WebP · 8 MB per image · 16 megapixels maximum. You can also select files from your Windows laptop.

Review before saving

Each detected amount starts as Skip this row. Choose its account, or create a new one. Check the amount against the original and enter the date Snoop last updated that account—not just today’s screenshot date. Credit-card debt must be negative.

Balances update your account overview and forecast starting points. They do not create transactions or a spending breakdown. Continue using CSV history for spending and cash-flow analysis.
