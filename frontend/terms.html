Personal installation · 25 September 2026

Personal planning tool

This self-hosted installation is for the owner's own records. SmartMoney is not affiliated with Snoop. Use only screenshots and records you are entitled to process.

Review required

OCR can misread amounts, names, signs and labels. Check selected accounts and actual balance refresh dates against the original before saving. The reader saves reviewed balance snapshots or spending summaries. It does not reconstruct individual transactions.

Estimates

Cash-flow projections and transfer suggestions depend on your saved balances, planned payments and assumptions. They may be incomplete or stale. Verify live available balances and obligations in your banking apps before acting. All transfers are performed manually outside SmartMoney.

Availability and data care

The browser must be able to reach the server. The screenshot reader runs locally after dependencies are installed; it does not provide offline bank updates. Protect server access and keep backups. Demo records are fictional. Stopping the service does not erase saved records or backups.
