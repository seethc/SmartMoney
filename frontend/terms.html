Personal installation · 23 September 2026

Purpose and operator

This SmartMoney installation is operated by its owner as a personal, self-hosted prototype for viewing and planning their own finances. It is not offered as a public or commercial service. The operator's contact is the data protection email supplied for this installation's application in the Enable Banking control panel.

Permitted use

Use this installation only for your own accounts and records, within the permissions you have authorised and the applicable bank/provider terms. Do not connect someone else's accounts or share access to their financial information through this personal installation. Review the application's operation and notices before expanding access or offering a service to others.

Read-only bank access

SmartMoney requests account information such as balances and transactions. It has no payment initiation, money-transfer execution, trading or bank-account management features. Any financial action must be performed by you in your banking or investment app.

Estimates and responsibility for decisions

Spending summaries depend on imported data and your classifications. Cash-flow projections and transfer suggestions are estimates based on recorded balances, schedules, buffers and allowances. They may omit payments, use stale data or contain errors. Confirm balances, obligations, account restrictions and transfer timing with your bank before acting. This prototype does not provide regulated financial advice or promise a financial outcome.

Availability and data care

The Pi and private network must remain available to use the app. Bank coverage, consent periods and provider availability can change. Sync is manual, and there is no promised service level or offline financial access. Keep backups, protect access credentials and maintain the Pi. Demo balances are fictional and must not be used as your real financial position.

Bank consent and third-party services

Your bank and Enable Banking handle bank authentication and consent under their own terms. Tailscale provides private network access under its terms. These SmartMoney terms do not replace or grant exceptions to any third-party terms. You can revoke bank access through the available app, bank or provider controls. Previously imported records remain locally after disconnection, as described in the privacy information.

Stopping use and changes

You can stop using the installation and revoke its bank access at any time. Stopping the service does not automatically erase stored records or backups. Review these terms when changing the application or how it is used.
